== STRATEGY ROADMAP ==

Level 1 - Heuristics:
  - Snake/zigzag weight pattern (keep biggest tile in corner)
  - Monotonicity (rows/columns in order)
  - Empty cell bonus (more = better, exponential weight)
  - Smoothness (penalize large differences between neighbors)
  - Merge potential (adjacent equal tiles)

Level 2 - Search:
  - Expectimax: evaluate future states considering random tile spawns
  - Adaptive depth: deeper when board is tight (≤4 empty cells → depth 4+)
  - Pruning: skip unlikely outcomes, sample empty cells when many

Level 3 - Advanced (for 95%→100%):
  - Transposition tables (cache evaluated positions)
  - Bitboard representation (encode board as 64-bit integer)
  - N-tuple networks with TD(0) learning
  - Move ordering for better pruning
  - Monte Carlo rollouts for position evaluation

== ANALYSIS TIPS ==

  - Score variance: high variance means inconsistent play
  - Speed: games/sec tells you if you can afford deeper search
  - Losing positions: what does the board look like at game over?
    Is your big tile trapped? Are you breaking monotonicity?
